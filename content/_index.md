I am a PhD student in Electrical Engineering at [Arizona State University](https://www.asu.edu/), advised by [Prof. Shaofeng Zou](https://sites.google.com/view/szou/home). I also work closely with [Prof. Sen Lin](https://slin70.github.io/) and [Prof. Yingbin Liang](https://sites.google.com/view/yingbinliang/home). Previously, I received my B.Eng. in Automation from [Xi’an Jiaotong University](https://www.xjtu.edu.cn/).

I am interested in **reinforcement learning, LLM post-training, and AI agents (including multi-agent systems)**, with a focus on safety, alignment, and decision-making under constraints. I am also interested in applications of these methods to healthcare.
